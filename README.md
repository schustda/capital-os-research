# Capital OS — Research Contact & Data Use

Capital OS is an independent financial research project investigating public discussions about stocks, speculative market activity, and investor attention patterns.

## Research Purpose

The project explores whether changes in social-media discussions, sentiment, and stock mentions can help identify emerging market activity.

Research may include publicly available content from Bluesky and other sources where collection and analysis are permitted.

Capital OS is not affiliated with Bluesky, InvestorsHub, or any brokerage.

## Data Collection and Privacy

The planned research system will:

- Collect only data relevant to its research objectives.
- Preserve publication and collection timestamps for accurate analysis.
- Store research observations privately rather than publishing a public message archive.
- Respect applicable platform requirements regarding content deletion and account removal.
- Limit data retention to what is necessary for the research.

Data collection is currently experimental and subject to source-specific access requirements.

## Contact and Data Removal

For research inquiries, privacy concerns, or requests to remove retained content, contact:

**capitalosresearch@gmail.com**

Please include the relevant post URL or identifier when possible.

Requests will be reviewed, and applicable removals will be processed in accordance with platform requirements.

## Project Status

Capital OS is an independently operated, noncommercial research project. It does not currently provide a public-facing social-media or trading service.
